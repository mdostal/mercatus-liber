Picking a winner (human decision)

This contact sheet and the 12 candidate PNGs are the review surface — selecting a final mark is deliberately left to a human, not decided by this generation pass. To record a selection, add selected.yaml to this same directory:

direction: 2      # integer, matches direction-<N>/ subdir
candidate: 1      # integer, 0..3, matches <i>.png inside that direction
notes: |          # optional free text
  Rationale for the pick goes here.
